PremiumRadio

PremiumRadio is an audio application that integrates with Google Drive to access audio files you explicitly select.

PremiumRadio helps you open, play, and manage audio files stored in your Google Drive. When you use the app, you choose which files to grant access to. PremiumRadio does not access any other files in your Google Drive, and it does not store your Google password.

The app uses the following Google OAuth scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. This scope allows PremiumRadio to access only the specific Google Drive files that you select or create through the app.
