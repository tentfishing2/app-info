Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how PremiumRadio ("the App", "we", "our", or "us") accesses and uses information when you use the App. PremiumRadio is an audio application that integrates with Google Drive to provide its core functionality.

2. Information We Access

With your permission, PremiumRadio accesses Google Drive files that you explicitly select or that you create or open through the App. The App uses the following Google OAuth scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. This permission allows the App to access specific Google Drive files that you choose to use with the App. It does not provide general access to all files in your Google Drive. We do not request or store your Google password.

3. How We Use Information

Information accessed through Google Drive is used only to provide the functionality of the App: opening audio files selected by you, playing or managing those files when requested by you, and saving changes to files through Google Drive. We do not sell Google user data or use it for advertising.

4. Data Storage and Security

PremiumRadio does not store Google Drive file contents or OAuth tokens on its servers. All access to Google Drive happens in real time through Google's API. We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the App against unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent personal information. Information is shared only when necessary to provide the App's functionality, comply with applicable law, or protect the rights and security of users and the App.

6. Google API Services

PremiumRadio's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Google's privacy policy is available at: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

7. Data Deletion

You may revoke PremiumRadio's access to your Google Account at any time through your Google Account security settings: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. To request deletion of any information stored by the App, contact us at asista7777@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

PremiumRadio is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or PremiumRadio's data practices, contact us at: asista7777@gmail.com
